MUSASHI Residence Morishita Station Front

MUSASHI Residence Morishita Station Front is conveniently located right in front of Morishita Station,
providing excellent access to major stations and tourist spots such as Tokyo Station
and Ginza Station. The Morishita area retains a nostalgic old-town atmosphere,with nearby shopping streets
and the Fukagawa Shinmei Shrine.The adjacent Kiyosumi-Shirakawa area is
also known for its art and coffee culture.

MUSASHI Residence Morishita Staition Frontは森下駅の目の前に位置しており、
東京駅や銀座駅などの主要駅や観光スポットへのアクセスにも最適です。
下町情緒の残る森下エリアは、周辺に商店街や深川神明宮、隣駅の清澄白河を中心に
アートやコーヒーの街としても人気があります。
