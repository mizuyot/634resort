MUSASHI Condo Yotsuya

MUSASHI Condo Yotsuya is located near significant landmarks such as the State Guest House,
the Imperial Palace, and the Crown Prince's Residence.
The area offers convenient access to major stations and features numerous restaurants along Shinjuku Dori
and Shinmichi Dori. This area is also home to many temples and shrines and has served as a filming location
for famous anime,providing a quintessentially Japanese and nostalgic feel.

MUSASHI Condo Yotsuyaは迎賓館、皇居や東宮御所にもほど近く、主要駅への交通アクセスにも便利な場所に位置しております。
新宿通りやしんみち通りをはじめ、飲食店が多く立ち並びます。周辺には神社仏閣も多く、
有名なアニメのロケ地にもなったりと日本ならではの庶民的で懐かしい顔をもつエリアです。
