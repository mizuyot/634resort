MUSASHI Condo Takadanobaba

MUSASHI Condo Takadanobaba is located along the Yamanote Line, providing easy access
to major stations without the need to transfer trains. The area is lively, with many young people
due to the nearby universities. It is also known as a hot spot for tonkatsu and ramen,
with a variety of restaurants lining the streets.The neighborhood blends the old and new,
offering retro ping pong and billiard halls alongside modern attractions.

MUSASHI Condo Takadanobabaは山手線沿線に位置しており、
主要駅までは乗換えなくアクセスに便利です。周辺に大学が多いことから若者が多く賑わいのあるこのエリアは、
とんかつとラーメンの激戦区とも言われ、様々な飲食店が立ち並びます。
レトロな卓球場やビリヤード場など新旧混交の雰囲気を楽しむことのできる街です。
