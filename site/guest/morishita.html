MUSASHI Residence Morishita

MUSASHI Residence Morishita is situated in the eastern part of Tokyo, in an area that retains the nostalgic
atmosphere of the old Edo period, once known as Fukagawa.The surrounding area features local shopping streets
and the Fukagawa Shinmei Shrine. The neighboring Kiyosumi-Shirakawa area is also popular for its art and coffee culture.
The residence provides convenient access to major stations and tourist spots: it’s a 7-minute train ride to Tokyo Station,
10 minutes to Ginza Station, and 20 minutes to Asakusa Temple and the Kabuki-za Theatre.

MUSASHI Residence Morishitaは東京の東側、江戸時代から深川と呼ばれ親しまれた下町情緒の残る場所に位置しております。
周辺には商店街や深川神明宮、隣駅の清澄白河を中心にアートやコーヒーの街としても人気があります。
電車を利用して東京駅まで7分、銀座駅まで10分、浅草寺や歌舞伎座までも20分と
主要な駅や観光スポットへのアクセスにも便利です。
