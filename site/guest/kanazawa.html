MUSASHI Condo Kanazawa

MUSASHI Condo Kanazawa is a 10-minute walk from Kanazawa Station, making it an ideal base for exploring the city.
Kanazawa offers a wealth of attractions, from fresh seafood from the Sea of Japan to art and architecture that inspire
the senses.The city's beautiful and charming streets are lined with traditional teahouse architecture and the
Teramachi Temple Area,home to around 70 temples. With a fully equipped kitchen and washer-dryer,
you can enjoy your stay in Kanazawa as if you were living there.

MUSASHI Condo Kanazawaは金沢駅から徒歩10分と金沢の旅に最適な場所に位置しております。
日本海の新鮮な海の幸をはじめ、アートや建築など感性に触れるスポットも多く、端正な茶屋建築が連なる茶屋街や、
約70もの寺院が集まる寺町寺院群など美しく情緒あふれる街並みが魅力です。
キッチンや洗濯乾燥機も完備しておりますので、金沢で暮らすように旅をお楽しみください。
