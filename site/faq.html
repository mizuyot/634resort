A - MUSASHI Condo Shinjuku, MUSASHI Residence Morishita, and MUSASHI Residence Morishita Station Front do not have parking. Please use nearby coin parking.
- MUSASHI Condo Yotsuya has one free parking space for a standard car on the first floor.

Q Can I bring pets?
A Pets and other animals are not allowed in any of the facilities. If pets are brought in without permission, there will be a penalty fee for any disruption to business.
Q Can minors stay alone?
A Minors are not allowed to stay alone. They must be accompanied by a parent or guardian.
Q Is there laundry service available during my stay?
A Each facility has a washing machine in every room. Some rooms also have dryers available.
Q Is smoking allowed in the hotel?
A Smoking is prohibited inside the buildings and rooms of all facilities. Smoking is also prohibited on the premises and nearby roads. At MUSASHI Condo Shinjuku, smoking is allowed only on the terrace. At MUSASHI Condo Takadanobaba, smoking is allowed only on the terrace and rooftop. At MUSASHI Condo Yotsuya, smoking is allowed only in the parking area where ashtrays are provided.
Q Is Wi-Fi available?
A Each room is equipped with Wi-Fi. Please note that the speed may slow down depending on the time and the number of devices being used.
Q What should I do with my trash?
A Please dispose of your trash in the bins provided in each room. For extended stays, trash collection may vary by facility, so please contact us. Large items or excessive trash that does not fit in the room's trash bin should be taken with you. If left behind, a disposal fee may apply. Please do not dispose of trash at nearby garbage stations.
Q What amenities are provided in the rooms?
A Amenities vary by room and facility. Please refer to the details page of each room for more information.
Q What should I do if I damage any items?
A If you damage any hotel property, please contact us immediately. If we are not informed or depending on the damage, you may be charged for the cost of the damaged item and any business losses.
Q Can I bring my own food and drinks?
A As we do not offer meal services, you are welcome to bring your own food and drinks.
Q Are there cooking utensils available?
A We provide plates, cups, chopsticks, and forks for the number of guests. Basic cooking utensils such as frying pans, pots, and knives are also provided.
Q What should I do with used cooking utensils?
A Please leave used cooking utensils and cutlery in the kitchen. You do not need to wash them, but for difficult stains or discoloration, please soak them in water to help with cleaning.

A We do not accept advance luggage delivery as there is no staff on-site. For same-day luggage storage, please contact us.
Q Can I order food delivery?
A You can use food delivery services if you can receive the order yourself in the room. We do not accept deliveries on behalf of guests. Please avoid using unattended delivery options as there are other guests.
